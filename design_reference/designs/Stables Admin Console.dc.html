Two kinds of account

This matrix covers console roles — your team. Separately, an app account is a person in stables: Toni is a trainer at Serc and at Emirates Park, with a role per stable.

A marketplace adds a third kind — a provider on the platform, who belongs to no stable and serves many. One person may be both. Build the account model with that dimension present even while the marketplace flag is off; adding it after a few hundred users is a migration.

Where this stands

Every stable is on Free while you grow. The three paid tiers are drafted but not published, so nothing bills and no card is ever asked for.

When you do publish one, the decision you have not made yet is who pays: the stable admin for the whole yard, or each rider for their own horses. The app is built either way — riders already belong to several stables with a different role in each.
