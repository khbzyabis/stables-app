My Stables — round 1

Onboarding, and the stable you land in

Sixty-seven screens: splash, sign in, sign up, verify, create your stable, home, add a horse, a horse profile, invites, accepting an invite, admin approvals, one account across several stables, a rider waiting on approval, the people list with roles, what each role can do, the week schedule, the month calendar, adding or editing an activity, a rider’s tack box, per-activity setups for a horse, a setup-changed alert, the kit a groom sees for a session, the noticeboard and posting to it, a horse’s health and training logs, the groom’s task list, assigning a task, who has done what, a filled-in horse profile that links to everything, the feed chart, documents, notifications, stable settings and leaving, your own profile, the stable’s contacts, the language picker, home and the noticeboard mirrored in Arabic, a groom’s day in Hindi, and the stable’s language default, help and reporting a problem, setting the stable’s location on a map, the Yard / Shows mode switch, editing a horse and moving her to another stable, day one with nothing in it, no signal in the barn, a declined card, a horse’s progress over time, and the buying side — market, an item, basket and checkout, an order with a way to raise a problem, asking several providers for a price, comparing what comes back, entering a show, a start list, and transport — requesting it, comparing what the companies come back with, the booked journey on the yard schedule, and everything you have paid for with its receipt. One direction, minimal containers — the page is mostly air, hairlines and type, with terracotta reserved for the single action on each screen and sage for status.

Assumptions I made: home leads with your horses, since that is what every role recognises first; the noticeboard sits one tap away on the tab bar. Sign-up collects name, email, phone and password, then verifies by SMS, then offers create-a-stable (you become admin) or join with an invite code. Both platforms share this layout; frames shown as iOS.

Interactive: password reveal, the create/join choice, and the home tab bar all work. Tap a screen's main button to jump to the next screen.
