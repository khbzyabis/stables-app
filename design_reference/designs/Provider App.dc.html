My Stables · the provider's phone

Hamad's day, one hand, dirty hands

Ten screens — three to apply, seven to work — and deliberately fewer than the web dashboard: today's work, a request to answer, a job being finished, the stable thread, orders to pack, and what he has earned. Everything else — listings, payouts, disputes, staff — stays on the desktop, where he has time to read.

Live: accept or decline a request, tick a job through its steps, and the earnings toggle. Buttons are 56px and up, because this is used standing in a barn.

What is deliberately not here

No listings editor, no payout ledger, no disputes, no staff permissions. A farrier between two yards should not be reading an arbitration thread on a phone, and adding an item properly needs photographs and measurements — both live on the web dashboard. The phone answers one question: what am I doing next, and did I get paid for the last one.
